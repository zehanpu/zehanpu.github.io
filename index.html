Ze Han

Email: hanze@pku.edu.cn

Welcome! I’m an Assistant Professor at the School of International Studies, Peking University.

My research focuses on global governance and the political economy of globalization.

I received my Ph.D. in Politics from Princeton University, where I was affiliated with the Niehaus Center for Globalization and Governance. My advisors were Helen Milner (chair), James Vreeland, and Rory Truex.

Before joining Peking University, I worked as a Data Scientist at the World Bank. I previously earned a B.A. in International Politics from the University of International Relations and, through the Campus Asia dual-degree program, an M.A. in Diplomacy from Peking University and a Master of Public Policy from the University of Tokyo, both with highest distinction.
